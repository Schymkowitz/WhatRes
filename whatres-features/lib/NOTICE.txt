Atom-parameter library: lib/<platform>/libatomparams.a

This library contains the FoldX atom parameters used for burial (per-atom
volume and minimal and maximal occupancy, FoldX 2014 parameter set) in
compiled form. It is not covered by the BSD licence in LICENSE.

It is distributed for use with whatres-features only, under the terms of the
FoldX licence. Do not decompile it, extract the parameter values, or
redistribute it separately from whatres-features.

FoldX: Schymkowitz J, Borg J, Stricher F, Nys R, Rousseau F, Serrano L. The
FoldX web server: an online force field. Nucleic Acids Res. 2005;33:W382-W388.
doi:10.1093/nar/gki387
